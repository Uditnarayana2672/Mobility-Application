Indore Spaces — maps and AR wayfinding for indoor places

Google-Maps-style navigation inside offices, malls, airports, stations and bus stands — with turn-by-turn on a 2D map, an AR mode with floor arrows, and ads on blank walls. Routing, instructions and AR are computed from the map data — no AI guessing. AI is used only for free-form voice questions.

2-minute demo script

  1. Print or open markers
    Open the Marker sheet — these are the stickers placed around the office.
  2. Open the live dashboard on the big screen
    Dashboard (or click “Open live dashboard” inside the visitor app).
  3. Scan marker M01 in the Visitor app
    Blue dot appears with a heading cone. Floor + position come from the marker.
  4. Say / type “Cafeteria kahan hai?” (🎤)
    Switch language to Hinglish or తెలుగు first. Then tap Directions.
  5. Compare stairs vs lift, tap Start
    Fastest = stairs (1 min). Toggle “Avoid stairs” for the accessible route.
  6. Tap 📷 for AR
    Floor chevrons, a yellow turn arrow, door signs and a video ad on a blank wall. Tap the ad.
  7. Watch the floor change
    “Taking the stairs…” → lobby marker confirms Floor 2 → arrival.
  8. Show the owner side
    In the Map editor rename a room → Publish → reload the visitor app. In Ads pause a campaign → ad vanishes in AR.
